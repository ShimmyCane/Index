Three proposed checks: test page

This page tests the three changes I'd suggest, against the checks we use today. It runs both on one small sample app, with its faults left in. Try the switch below, then tick the ones you want built.

Where the improvement shows. Today's check looks for JavaScript errors, sideways scrolling and whether controls respond. It does not look at colour contrast, the six control states, or touch size and fallbacks. So the sample below passes today's check except for the sideways scroll, but still has problems the proposals would catch.

Important: the sideways-scroll result for today's check depends on the window width. Open this page on a phone (390 px wide) to see that check fail. On a desktop it passes.

4. The three proposals, in plain words

A. Contrast check (from tastemaker)

What: for each piece of text, read its colour and the colour behind it, compute the ratio, and fail anything under the rule. Run it in light and dark mode.

Where: a new step 9 in the smoke test. Suggested fix shown in table 3.

Cost: a few seconds per page. Risk: a text colour that passes can still look poor; it is a check, not taste.

B. Six control states (from dembrandt)

What: every main button must look different in rest, hover, focus, active, disabled and loading. A button that looks disabled must not respond to clicks.

Where: a new step 10 in the smoke test.

Cost: a short extra pass per page. Risk: hover does not exist on a touch screen, so hover is only judged on desktop.

C. Touch and phone gates (from forever-ai-components)

What: three gates. Every tap target is at least 48 px tall. Every drag-only control has a button alternative. Nothing scrolls sideways inside a panel at phone width.

Where: a new gate section in the smoke test, next to step 3 (touch).

Cost: small. Risk: a drag-only control in a game may be fine with a keyboard instead; say so in the page rather than failing it.
