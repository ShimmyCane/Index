## Scale used in the model (estimates, exaggerated, NOT to scale)
- Real sizes and distances range over a factor of more than 10,000. The model compresses them so every body can be seen.
- Sun radius 40 in (stylised). Planets are exaggerated: small planets 6 to 10 in, gas giants 14 to 22 in, Pluto 3.5 in.
- Orbit radii are compressed (not to scale): Mercury 90 in, Venus 120, Earth 150, Mars 190, asteroid belt 240 to 290, Jupiter 360, Saturn 450, Uranus 530, Neptune 600, Pluto 690 (tilted orbit).
- Ratios that are kept: the order of the planets, the gas giants being the largest, Saturn's rings, Venus and Uranus spinning the other way, and Pluto being the smallest.
- Speeds are stylised: each planet completes a whole number of laps per 240 s loop, in the same order as the real periods (Mercury fastest, Pluto slowest). Whole laps are needed so the loop closes.
- Spin periods are stylised too (whole turns per loop), with the same order as the real spins.

## Where sources disagree
- Charon's radius: 1,212 km (Wikipedia) vs 1,208 km (NASA StarChild). Model uses about 1,210 km; not visible at this scale.
- Pluto's radius: 1,188 km (NASA fact sheet) vs 1,180 km (JPL education PDF). The NASA fact sheet is used.
- Moon counts change as new moons are found. The model shows only the main moons.
