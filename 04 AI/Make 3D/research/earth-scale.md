# Earth V22: scale of the satellites, rockets, storms and clouds

**Subject:** Earth (planet view), increment V22. The floating parts (storms, clouds, satellites, rockets) brought closer to scale, with faded orbit lines and about ten times the satellites.
**Mode:** research (figures from the usual published sizes; recorded here, not copied from pages).

## Scale used

Earth's radius is 9.5 in in the model (6,371 km), so 1 in is about 670 km.

| Thing | Real size or height | In the model | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | radius 6,371 km | 9.5 in radius | the reference |
| ISS | about 109 m long, about 408 km up | box 0.4 in with 0.6 in panels, about 1.8 in above the ground | exaggerated about 4 times in size; height stretched about 3 times |
| Hubble | about 13 m long, about 540 km up | 0.3 in body with panels, about 2.3 in above the ground | larger than to scale |
| LEO satellites | 10 m-class, 400 to 1,000 km up | 68 small spheres (0.25 in) on four rings, 2.5 to 3.4 in above the ground | constellation, about 17 per ring |
| Navigation ring | about 20,200 km up | 6 spheres, 7.5 in above the ground | GPS-like; real height is about 30 in |
| Geostationary ring | about 35,786 km up | 4 cubes, 14.5 in above the ground | turns 32 times per loop, the same as Earth, so it stays over one spot |
| Polar weather satellite | about 800 km up, polar | box 0.25 in, 4.2 in above the ground | |
| Rockets | about 70 m tall | 0.85 in tall (stage 0.6 in, nose 0.25 in) | exaggerated about 1,000 times; it must be seen |
| Launch pads | about 100 m across | 0.7 in across | |
| Storms (hurricanes) | about 300 to 1,000 km across | about 2.5 in across (about 1,700 km) | two to three times their size |
| Cloud layer | about 10 km thick | shells 0.1 in and 0.2 in above the ground (about 70 and 130 km) | exaggerated so they read as cloud |
| Moon orbit | 384,400 km | 30 in from the centre (about 20 in above the ground) | not changed in V22; not to scale |

## Order kept

LEO (ISS, Hubble, LEO rings) < navigation ring < geostationary ring < the Moon. The rings sit at 10 to 12, 17, 24 and 30 in from the centre.

## Satellites

- About 81 satellites in V22, against 8 in V21 (about ten times): 68 LEO spheres, 6 navigation, 4 geostationary, the ISS, Hubble and the polar satellite.
- Each ring is a group that turns about its normal (x, y or z). The orbit lines are faded tubes on the same planes (opacity 0.3, so taps pass through).
- Turn counts per 480 s loop are whole numbers (the orbit speeds are stylised, as the rest of the model).

## Limits

- James Webb (about 1.5 million km away, at the Sun-Earth L2 point) is not drawn: at this scale it would be off the page.
- Sizes are exaggerated as stated above; the ISS and Hubble are not drawn to the same ratio as the real ones.
- The mountains (V12, olive cones) are not part of this increment.
