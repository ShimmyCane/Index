# Better Parenting — lessons

## 2026-11-16 (first build)
1. **Two axes serve "specific and general" at once.** Topic × stage gives the general view (a plan per topic with one note per stage); scenarios hang under it; an own-scenario builder reuses the same topic plan. One content model, three ways in.
2. **Keep a child's details out of content by making them a setting.** "My kids' grades" lives on the device and drives a filter. The repo holds nothing about the family.
3. **Only search-found video links.** The sandbox cannot open YouTube, so ids from memory cannot be checked. Use ids returned by a search, label them unwatched, and add a search link everywhere as the fallback.
4. **Practice without writing three wrong answers per item:** answer first, reveal, self-rate, and bring misses back sooner. It needs no extra content.
5. **A data check belongs in the smoke test.** One `page.evaluate` confirms every scenario's topic, stage and skill ids exist and no id repeats.
6. **The Supabase tool can stop for a confirmation in a remote session** even for a rolled-back `do $$` test. Nothing ran. Keep the paste files ready and tell Jimmy.
