# Design interests (public, simplified)

The designer's personal details live in a private file. This version keeps only the game-design interests that shape Expansion Wars and later projects.

## Game styles that appeal

- **Expansion and empire-building:** Mushroom Wars, Civilization, Colonization, Age of Empires.
- **Zooming between scales:** the whole map and the individual units. Total War is the model: the overall map links to the battles.
- **Base and resource play** over fast, twitchy combat.
- **Party and puzzle adventure:** Baldur's Gate-style parties, Zelda-style puzzles, Tomb Raider-style exploration.
- **Management and sims:** Roller Coaster Tycoon, Dungeon Keeper, farm sims.
- **Education that is game first:** Oregon Trail.
- **Slower, tabletop-style digital games** with deliberate turns.

## Mechanics to explore

- **Counter systems:** a triangle of strengths and weaknesses (pikemen vs. cavalry, archers vs. swordsmen, catapults vs. walls), and the many-type matchups of Pokémon.
- **Strategy theory:** *The Art of War* as a reference for positioning and tempo.
- **Simple rules first:** start with a few rules (spots, soldiers, sending, capture) and add mechanics one at a time.

## Design takeaways for Expansion Wars

- Keep the first rules small. Add one mechanic per increment.
- Let the map and the individual units connect, so a player can see both.
- Plan counter systems as a later increment, once the basic expansion loop works.
