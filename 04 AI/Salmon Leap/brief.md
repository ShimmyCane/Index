# Salmon Leap: brief

A bronze statue of an Atlantic salmon leaping up, in five versions, from narrative and massing to the ground. Movement, rendering and atmosphere (V10) and sound (V15) come later.

## State (2026-11-18)

- V1 to V5 built and audited (15, 17, 26, 32, 40 parts). Test: `node tests/test_versions.js`.
- Page: `index.html` (versions, narrative, views including back, colour modes).
- Not yet: V10 (movement, rendering, atmosphere), V15 (sound). Not device-tested.

## Decisions

- Name: "Salmon Leap" (the project name was not given; chosen for the statue). Change if you prefer another.
- Track B (studio design). Inches, Z up. The fish moves in the x-z plane; the nose is at +x.
