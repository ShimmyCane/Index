when I ask to build a website, I'm thinking of a play thing for the kids. On ipad. Large buttons.

Color scheme - light pastel
Graphics - simple, but not too wireframe like. Use bold lines, graphically make it interesting
Focus - minimize menus, settings, texts. Keep main item accessible. 
Use - ipad.
Settings - a gear on the lower right button that allows further extensions.

Layout (side by side where space allows - laptops and iPads in landscape):
- Two columns, each scrolling independently; the page itself does not scroll (app fills the viewport height). They don't have to be evenly sized.
- Left (fixed, the "play" side): the main play object (e.g. the table/board) is always visible. Draggable items/pieces sit on top; the key numbers and primary actions (e.g. amount, Surprise me, Show me one, Undo, status) sit on the bottom.
- Right (scrollable): reference content - views, strategies, progress - as stacked panels with +/- toggles (several open at once, never overwriting each other).
- Narrow or short screens (portrait, small windows): collapse to a single column, play side first.

Settings / decluttering:
- On limited space, hide secondary controls (e.g. coin-type choices, mode toggles) behind a settings button; closed by default when space is tight, open by default on large screens.
- Everything hidden must stay reachable through settings; never remove a feature just to save space.

where - it should be within the project folder.

Other - screen areas/ interactive divs and elements shouldn't resize drastically in a way that causes the items below to jump around. Other than show/hide of course.
