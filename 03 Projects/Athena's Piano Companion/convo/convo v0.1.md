# Athena's Piano Companion — Convo v0.1

**Project:** Athena's Piano Companion
**Round:** first questions, 2026-11-20. Nothing is built yet.

## What I heard

Athena is learning piano, and her best moments were free play. Felix is inspired to jam too. You want a companion page, not an app, that listens with her. She keeps her hands on the piano and runs it by voice. She says something like "start recording" or "I want to record something", a beep-beep-beep-bop countdown plays, and she records. When she pauses for about three seconds the page blinks, which is the signal that the clip has stopped, so she never has to say "stop". An alternative stop is a chosen key or short motif; the page then cuts the last couple of seconds. Then it is ready for the next clip, so she can build a series of clips, then leave the piano and work with them.

## Questions

1. **Track.** Family play (few words, big shapes, everything else in ⚙️), I assume. Right? *(recommended: yes)*
   - Answer:
2. **Device and piano.** Which device sits at the piano (iPad, laptop, phone)? Is it an acoustic piano or a digital one? A digital piano with a USB or Bluetooth MIDI link would be far more reliable than a microphone, but iPad Safari does not support Web MIDI, so on an iPad the microphone is the route. *(recommended: microphone first; MIDI as a later option)*
   - Answer:
3. **Start phrase.** Browser speech recognition can listen for phrases, but it needs internet and may not work in iPad Safari, and it can confuse piano sound for speech. Proposal: voice start with a big touch circle always available as a backup. Which phrases: "start recording", "I want to record", "let's record"? Any others, or a short name for it, such as "Hey Piano"? *(recommended: those three plus a name you pick)*
   - Answer:
4. **Countdown.** Three low beeps and a higher fourth, with three dots filling on screen. Recording starts on the high beep. Fine? Should the count be skippable (start at once)? *(recommended: as proposed, no skip)*
   - Answer:
5. **Stopping.** Three ways, each switchable in ⚙️: (a) about 3 seconds of silence, with a blink before it ends; (b) a stop key or motif; (c) the word "stop". I propose (a) and (b) on, (c) off. For (b), which gesture: the lowest A three times, a cluster of both hands at the top, or one you pick on first use ("play your stop sound now")? *(recommended: learn the stop gesture on first use)*
   - Answer:
6. **Silence length.** 3 seconds, adjustable 2–6 in ⚙️. Free play has pauses to think. Should the blink warn for 1 second, and playing again cancel the stop? *(recommended: yes)*
   - Answer:
7. **Snipping.** Should the end of the take be trimmed to just after her last note, with a short natural fade, and the start trimmed to just before her first note (with a small pre-roll so no note is clipped)? *(recommended: yes, both)*
   - Answer:
8. **After the take.** Chime, then the take appears as a card in a strip and the page is ready again. What should the card show: a number, a colour, a small waveform? Should the page name takes automatically ("Take 1", or the date)? *(recommended: colour, waveform and "Take N")*
   - Answer:
9. **Away from the piano.** What should she be able to do with takes? Choices: play, star, rename, delete (with undo), trim, loop, layer two takes, export as a file. Which two or three first? *(recommended: play, star, delete with undo; then trim and layer)*
   - Answer:
10. **Keeping recordings.** Takes stay on the device in the browser, never uploaded. The risk is that clearing browser data loses them. Add an Export button (a file saved to the device)? Or do you want a family-database bucket later, as in Our Family Compass? *(recommended: device and export now; database later if you ask)*
    - Answer:
11. **Eyes and gestures.** Camera eye-watching is a lot of work and raises privacy questions. I suggest leaving it out. Agree? *(recommended: leave out)*
    - Answer:
12. **Listening as a listener.** You mentioned it is delightful to listen as well. Should the page have a "play all takes in a row" mode, a shuffle, or a "show only starred" mode for listeners? *(recommended: play-all and starred-only later, not first build)*
