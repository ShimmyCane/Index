How to read this. What means: standing at the object, you can say what it holds. Where means: given the name, you can point to its room. They are counted apart, because knowing the way is not the same as knowing the thing. A right answer waits longer before it comes back (1, 2, 4, 8, then 16 days). A miss comes back today.

To move things: turn on Move below. Drag an object to another spot or another room. Drag a floor to move the room. Drag the ball in the middle of a world to move the world. Connections stay. Undo takes back any change.

Rooms grow by themselves: Nook, Room (3), Hall (6), Library (10), Tower (16). Size shows how much is kept there, not how well you know it. A gold ring on a stand shows what you have practised.

Each person has their own palace. It saves itself on this device after every change.

A copy is one JSON file with everything in it: worlds, rooms, memories, connections and practice history. Opening a copy or the sample can be undone.
