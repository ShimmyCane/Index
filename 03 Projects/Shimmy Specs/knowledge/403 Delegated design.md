Written in our own words. Points marked verify still need checking against a current CSI publication.

## 4. Delegated design

- When the contractor's engineer designs part of the work (anchors, curtain wall, stairs, sealant joints, shoring), say so in Part 1 (**Delegated-Design Services**) and Part 2 (**Performance Requirements**), require signed and sealed submittals, and coordinate with the structural engineer.
