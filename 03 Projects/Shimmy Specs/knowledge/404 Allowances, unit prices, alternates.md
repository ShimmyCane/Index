# Chapter 404 — Allowances, unit prices, alternates

Written in our own words. Points marked verify still need checking against a current CSI publication.

## 5. Allowances, unit prices, alternates

- **Allowances** for scope the design can't fix yet (e.g. "Allowance for 200 sq. ft. of concrete repair").
- **Unit prices** for unknown quantities (repair work lives on these: repointing per sq. ft., brick replacement per unit, crack repair per lin. ft.). Define the unit, the measurement method and what is included.
- **Alternates** for priced choices (base vs. deduct/add). Describe both conditions completely.
- Each needs an entry in Division 01 *and* a cross-reference in the affected section's Part 1.
