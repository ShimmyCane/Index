# Chapter 407 — Risk and language

Written in our own words. Points marked verify still need checking against a current CSI publication.

## 8. Risk and language

- Avoid **"as directed"**, **"to the satisfaction of the Architect"**: they make the architect the judge without criteria. State the criteria.
- Avoid **guarantees you can't measure**: "watertight" with no test method.
- **Warranties:** special warranties (manufacturer and installer) add to the General Conditions warranty; state the term, start, what is covered, and what isn't.
