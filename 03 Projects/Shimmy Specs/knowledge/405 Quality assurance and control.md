# Chapter 405 — Quality assurance and control

Written in our own words. Points marked verify still need checking against a current CSI publication.

## 6. Quality

- **QA** (before/around the work): qualifications, mockups, preconstruction testing, preinstallation meetings.
- **QC** (checking the work): field tests, inspections, special inspections (IBC Ch. 17), manufacturer's field service.
- Say **who pays** for testing and **who hires** the agency, in Division 01.
