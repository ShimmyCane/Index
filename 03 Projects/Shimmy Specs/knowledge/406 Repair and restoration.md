# Chapter 406 — Repair and restoration

Written in our own words. Points marked verify still need checking against a current CSI publication.

## 7. Specifying for repair and restoration

Repair work turns a lot of the normal rules around:

- **Existing conditions are the design.** Rely on surveys, probes, condition assessments, testing (mortar analysis, petrography, core samples, hazardous materials). Reference reports as information, not as part of the contract, unless intended **(verify with counsel)**.
- **Quantities are unknown** → unit prices and allowances, with a clear way to measure and verify.
- **Mockups and test panels** set the standard (cleaning methods, repointing, patch color/texture). Require approval before full work starts; keep them as the reference.
- **Compatibility first:** new material must not harm old (mortar softer and more permeable than the masonry; sealants compatible with substrates and coatings; cleaning agents tested).
- **Historic work:** Secretary of the Interior's Standards (preservation, rehabilitation, restoration, reconstruction) and NPS Preservation Briefs. `01 35 91 Historic Treatment Procedures`.
- **Occupied buildings:** phasing, noise and dust, protection, access, hours: `01 10 00`, `01 35 16`, `01 50 00`.
- **Hazardous materials:** lead, asbestos, PCBs in old sealants: Division 02 sections and owner surveys.
- **Sections to know:** `02 41 19 Selective Demolition`, `xx 01 00` maintenance sections (`03 01 30`, `04 01 20`, `05 01 10`, `07 01 50`, `08 01 52`… **(verify each number)**).
