# Base sections — a starting catalog

Common sections by division, with the **drivers** that bring them in or leave them out. This is the seed for the app's section recommender. It is **not** the full MasterFormat list (that is CSI's copyrighted product); numbers and titles here are the common ones, written down for our own use. Numbers marked **(verify)** need checking against the current edition.

Legend: **R** = nearly always required · **D** = driver-dependent · **Repair** = typical in repair/restoration work

## Example: how drivers prune the list

**Synthetic project:** six-story apartment building (R-2), Type IIB, CMU bearing walls with brick veneer, sprinklered, **façade repair and window replacement**, occupied during work.

- **In:** 01 basics + `01 22 00`, `01 35 16`, `01 43 39`; `02 41 19`; `02 83 00` if lead found; `04 01 20`, `04 05 19` (helical ties), `05 01 10`/`05 50 00` (lintels); `07 19 00` (if tested), `07 62 00`, `07 92 00`; `08 51 13`, `08 80 00`; `09 91 13`.
- **Out, with reasons:** most of 06 (noncombustible type, no framing in scope; keep `06 10 53` only if blocking at new windows); 03 30 00 (no new concrete); 09 interior finishes (exterior scope only).
- **Consider:** `03 01 30` (balcony slab edges?), `07 01 50` (roof work at parapets?), `07 27 00` (air-barrier continuity at new windows).
