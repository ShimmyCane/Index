# Chapter 402 — Products and substitutions

Written in our own words. Points marked verify still need checking against a current CSI publication.

## 3. Product selection and substitutions

- **Basis of design:** name one product as the design basis, list others as acceptable, and set the criteria a substitute must meet.
- **Substitution procedure** (`01 25 00`): when (during bidding vs. after award), what the contractor must submit (comparison, cost, schedule, warranty, effect on other work), what the architect will do, and the time limits.
- **Equal is not same:** a substitution must meet the *performance and compatibility* needs, not only the data sheet.
