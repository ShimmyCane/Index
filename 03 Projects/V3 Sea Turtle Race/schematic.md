# V3 Sea Turtle Race — schematic

**Track: A. Family play.** One of the animal games in the series (V1 is the Cheetah Simulator). Status (2026-11-25): **started, nothing built.** Clarifying questions are in `convo/convo v0.1.md`.

## Purpose

Race. Each animal's game is built around how it really lives. Facts are in `research/` (to be written); facts marked ⚑ are unchecked.

Mechanic family: **Race**, shared with the other games in the series.

## Controls

Follow `04 AI/Skills/game_controls.md`: drag relative to the centre by default (Felix), joystick in ⚙️ (lower left), action buttons lower right, ⚙️ upper right and fading. Proposed verbs: drag left or right to steer across the beach; a dash button near the sea.

## Gentle and mature

- **Gentle (on by default):** Hatchlings reach the sea; the gentle version says some get away.
- **Mature:** Hatchlings are taken by birds and crabs, and the facts say how few reach adulthood ⚑.
- The facts never change, only the telling (the "How it really is" panel).

## Settings: jump between games

Every game in the series has an **Other games** drop-down in ⚙️ that links to the others (V1 Cheetah Simulator, V2 Gibbon Swing, V3 Sea Turtle Race, V4 Golden Eagle Dive). A game that is not built yet is shown but disabled until its page exists.
