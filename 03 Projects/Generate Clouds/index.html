      Skies right now

        What this is not: a photograph of the real place. The amounts, heights, wind, sun and haze follow the data. The exact shape of each cloud is generated. The ground is a coarse height map (one point every 5 km) with made-up small relief, so coasts and hills are in roughly the right place and nothing more; buildings and trees are not drawn.

        Sample skies, one per cloud type. They use the place and date above, with made-up weather.

        Start from the data, then add weather by hand. Tornadoes, waterspouts and microbursts are never in the data: they are drawn from descriptions.

        A funnel or microburst is placed 5 to 9 km ahead of where you are looking. Press play to see it move.

        Shuffle keeps the weather and deals new cloud shapes and a new mix of types.

        Start from the data, then change it by hand. A changed value is marked ✎.

        Pictures are drawn at a higher quality than the moving view, at the moment shown on the time bar. The panorama is equirectangular (2:1), north in the middle, ready for a sky dome or HDRI slot.

        Settings

        Fine is slower. The view lowers its sharpness by itself while the time lapse plays, to stay smooth. When the view is still, it is redrawn sharp with fine detail; zoom in to see the wisps.

        Sky covered is how much sky the time lapse shows. Playback is how long one run takes in real time. Speed is the sky covered divided by the playback: 1× is real time, 0.5× is half time.

        Faint lines and names of fifteen constellations, where they are in the sky now. The bright stars are always drawn at night.

        Pick a mode in the bottom bar. Look (the default) turns the view with one finger. On the ground, Walk moves you like a map. In the air, Orbit a cloud, then tap a cloud to circle it. High up, Pan moves the view across the Earth; when the drag ends, the centre becomes the new place, and Clouds turns the clouds on or off. Two fingers turn the view at any height; pinch or scroll zooms.

        Orbit circles a point in the clouds. Drag to go around and over it, pinch to come closer. Turn goes around twice by itself, then waits.

        Starts after you press play once. The rumble arrives late, as in life: 3 seconds for each kilometre.

        Hides the play, time and height controls so the picture has the whole screen. The ⚙️ stays. Show brings them back.

        Hide the clouds to look at the land and sea underneath, from the ground or from space.

        Makes hills and valleys taller than they are, so gentle land is easier to read. The sea is not changed.
