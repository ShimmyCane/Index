## Questions

1. **First look.** Which sample in the Cloud atlas is closest to what you had in mind, and which is furthest? A note in ⚙️ on each is enough.

2. **Real sky or believable sky?** The MVP matches the amounts, heights, wind and light, and invents the shapes. Do you want the next step to place clouds where they really are (satellite images as the map from above), or is a believable sky for that weather the goal?

3. **Backdrop or light source?** The saved pictures are 8-bit PNG, good as a backdrop. Do you also want an HDR file that can light a rendered scene? Which program will receive it?

4. **Moving through the sky.** Now: look around and change height. Next could be (a) a flight path that loops, (b) driving under the clouds along a road direction, (c) free flight with a joystick. Which one first?

5. **Time lapse.** 15 minutes in 15 seconds, looping. Do you also want a whole day (sunrise to dusk in one minute), or a saved video or frame sequence?

6. **The ground.** It is a flat blue-grey plane. Sea, flat land, real terrain from elevation data, or leave it out of the pictures?

7. **The moon and night.** Night shows stars and dark clouds. Is a moon with moonlit clouds worth a round?

8. **Learning.** The reading panel explains each sky. Would you like a separate "field guide" page (the ten cloud types, each drawn live, with the research notes in plain words), and should it be written for the kids as well?

9. **Saving skies.** Should a sky you like be kept as a recipe (place, time, adjustments, camera) in a list on the device, with a link you can reopen?

10. **Device.** Which device will you use most: iPad, phone or computer? The picture quality default depends on it.

11. **Live data check.** The build sandbox could not reach the weather service, so the first real call happens on your device. In ⚙️, "Check the data link" should show three ✅. Please tell me what the pill at the top says after "Generate this sky".
