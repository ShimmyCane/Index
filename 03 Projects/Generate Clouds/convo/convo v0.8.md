## Note 2: stars and constellations ("basic constellations. Stars and suns")

Plan: a list of about 300 of the brightest stars (position, brightness, colour from the star's type: Betelgeuse and Antares red, Rigel and Spica blue-white, the Sun-like ones yellow) and about 25 constellation figures. They are placed in the night sky from the real time and latitude, so Orion is where it really is for the place and moment, the Southern Cross shows from the south, and they turn through the night.

1. **Which constellations?** My start: Orion, Ursa Major (the Big Dipper), Cassiopeia, Crux (Southern Cross), Scorpius, Leo, Gemini, Taurus, Cygnus, Lyra, Aquila, Canis Major, Canis Minor, Centaurus, Pegasus. Others?
2. **Names and lines?** Should the page draw faint lines between the stars and show names, only when you ask (a setting), or never?
3. **"Suns":** did you mean the bright stars as distant suns (coloured by type), or also a way to show our own Sun's path through the day?
4. **Data source:** star positions I will take from a public catalogue and check against a second source; I do not want to type them from memory. Is that fine, and is a night-sky image credit acceptable?
