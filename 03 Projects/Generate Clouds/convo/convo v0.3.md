## Said plainly

Tornadoes are not drawn. The weather data gives thunderstorms and how unstable the air is, not rotation or funnels, and the cloud model has no wind inside a single storm. What you can circle are tall storm towers with anvils.

## Questions

2. **Real cells.** Should radar be the next data source, so storm towers stand where the rain really is?
3. **Orbit centre.** Now it is where you were looking. Would you rather tap a cloud to circle it?
