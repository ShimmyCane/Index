# Felix Maze — Inspirations

**Status:** first pass, 100 entries. Draft for discussion in `convo/convo v0.1.md`.
**Purpose:** a broad survey of where mazes can come from, so Felix sees many kinds of maze and doesn't settle on one idea of what a maze is. Each entry can become one or more printed mazes. The "Inspired by" text is the seed for the snippet at the bottom of each sheet.

## How to read the table

Each row is tagged so the list can be sorted and filtered (see "Sorted views" below).

**Family:** where the idea comes from.
`HIST` classical and historical · `FAME` famous real mazes · `ART` art and artists · `NAT` nature (animals, plants, geology, geography) · `SYS` systems, networks, maps, machines · `MATH` geometry and patterns · `MUS` music, sound and frequencies · `STORY` characters and story worlds

**Geometry:** the shape of the maze underneath.
`GRID` square cells · `HEX` hexagon cells · `TRI` triangle cells · `POLAR` rings and spokes · `SPIRAL` · `ORGANIC` free curves, no grid · `SILH` maze filling a silhouette (an animal, a letter) · `NET` dots and lines, like a map · `TILE` repeating tiles · `FRACT` fractal or space-filling curve · `UNI` one path, no choices (a labyrinth)

**Line:** how the walls and paths are drawn.
`THIN` thin walls · `BOLD` thick hand-drawn walls · `SOLID` filled walls, white path · `RIBBON` the path is a drawn road or river · `DOUBLE` double-line roads or hedges · `DOTS` stipple or dots · `HATCH` texture fill (lines, scales, bricks)

**Density:** `L` big paths, few turns · `M` · `H` many small paths (older kids, or poster mode)

**Mechanic:** an extra idea beyond "find the way". `—` none · `BRIDGE` paths cross over and under · `ONEWAY` arrows · `GATE` keys and doors · `RULE` follow a color, number or pattern rule · `LAYER` stairs or levels · `PORTAL` jump from one spot to another · `HIDDEN` the solved path draws a picture · `COLLECT` pick things up on the way

## Notes

- Art references stay "inspired by": the maze borrows the shapes and the feeling, not the painting. Paintings before about 1930 (Van Gogh, Hokusai, Klimt) are public domain. Living or recent artists (Haring, Kusama, Riley) are style references only. No traced images.
- Cultural designs (5 Man in the Maze, 46 sand mandala) are named with respect and used as loose inspiration. Don't copy them exactly.
- Brands (arcade games, toy names) appear only as generic styles.
