# Felix Maze — Variations

**Status:** first pass. A menu of knobs the generator can turn, so no two mazes feel the same.
**How to use it:** each maze sheet is one inspiration (`inspirations.md`) plus a few choices from each section below. A sheet's recipe is saved with it, so it can be remade or tweaked.

## 1. Geometry (the shape underneath)

- Square cells, hexagons, triangles, rings and spokes, spirals
- No cells: free curves that grow and wiggle (organic)
- A maze clipped to a silhouette: an animal, a letter, a rocket, Felix's own doodle
- Repeating tiles (Truchet, Celtic, Islamic stars)
- Fractal and space-filling curves (Hilbert, dragon)
- Mixed regions: different textures in different parts of one picture (swirl sky, straight village)
- Outer frame: rectangle, circle, the full sheet edge, or no frame at all (the picture is the edge)

## 2. Routing and difficulty

- Labyrinth (one path) for warm-up, then a true maze
- Perfect (one solution) or braided (loops, fewer dead ends)
- Texture: long and winding vs. short and spiky (the growing-tree setting)
- Size: cell size and count
- Number of dead ends and decoys
- Solution length: short, or snaking through most of the sheet
- Several starts or finishes ("which animal gets home?")

## 3. Line weight and stroke

- Thin pen, medium marker, thick brush
- Even width, or swelling and thinning like a brush stroke
- Straight and exact, or hand-wobbled with rounded corners
- Single line, double line (hedges, roads, pipes), triple line (rails)
- Broken lines: dashes, stitches, dots, beads on a string
- Wall ends: square, round, flared, little leaves or stars on the tips

## 4. Walls vs. paths

- Draw the walls (the classic look)
- Draw the path (a road, river, vine, tunnel); everything else is background
- Solid walls with a white path (caves, tunnels, earth)
- Textured walls: bricks, hedge leaves, stone, waves, fur, scales, stars
- Walls that are things: a row of trees, a line of fence posts, a snake's body, a crowd of figures

## 5. Tone and color (printed in black and white)

The printer is black and white, so color becomes pattern.

- Patterns for shades: dots (light), stripes (medium), crosshatch (dark), solid black (darkest, small areas only)
- A pattern can be a rule: "only walk on striped tiles"
- Gray tones: light gray for background so the maze lines stand out
- White space on purpose: big shapes left blank to color in after solving
- Later, color versions for screen or a color printer

## 6. Mechanisms (on paper)

- **Bridges and tunnels:** paths pass over and under. Mark the crossing clearly.
- **One-way arrows:** rivers, slides, lava, conveyor belts
- **Keys and doors:** pick up the key before the door. Gates shown as a lock.
- **Portals:** matching symbols jump you across the sheet (two stars, two rabbit holes)
- **Levels:** stairs or ladders to a second map on the same page
- **Color or pattern rule:** only step on stripes, then dots, then stripes
- **Number rule:** count by 1s, 2s or 5s along the right path
- **Collect on the way:** cheese crumbs, acorns, stars; count how many you got
- **Avoid:** don't pass the sleeping goblin, cross the river only at a bridge
- **Race:** two players, two starts, one finish

## 7. Characters and story

- A start and finish that belong together: mouse and cheese, bee and flower, ant and crumb, rocket and moon, goblin and treasure, knight and castle
- Creatures along the way (helpers or things to avoid)
- A one-line story at the top ("The ant needs to bring the crumb home before rain")
- A recurring guide character for the whole series (to decide)

## 8. Composition

- The maze is the whole picture (Starry Night sky)
- The maze sits inside a picture (a maze in the snail's shell)
- **Hidden picture:** the right path draws a shape when traced (cornfield, one-line drawing)
- Small "zoomed-in" bonus maze in a corner
- Portrait or landscape on 11×17

## 9. Worksheet extras

- Title "Felix Maze #001" and the maze name
- Footer: where it's from and who inspired it, in one or two lines
- "Solved by ______ on ______" line, and a time-to-solve box
- Difficulty stars (1 to 5)
- A small "find these" list: three things hidden in the picture
- Color it in after solving
- Answer page (separate, or small and upside down)

## 10. Made with Felix

- **Doodle to maze:** Felix draws a few lines; the computer makes mazes from them. Possible meanings of the lines (to decide in convo):
  - the outline the maze fills (silhouette)
  - the right path (the maze grows around it)
  - big walls or rivers the maze must respect
- The computer offers 3 candidates; Felix picks one, names it and "bakes" it. It joins the numbered series.
- **Felix's paper mazes:** photograph one, trace it, keep its feel as a style (only if Jimmy wants his drawings stored).
- **Felix edits:** move the start, add a goblin, make a wall thicker.
