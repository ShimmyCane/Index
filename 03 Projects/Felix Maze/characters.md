# Felix Maze — Characters

The cast that walks through the mazes. Drawings live in `js/art.js` (one function per character, drawn in a 100 × 100 box). Add a row here whenever a character is added or changed.

**Rules:** friendly, original drawings, bold lines that print well in black and white. No scary faces, no weapons, no brands or cartoon characters from TV or books. Every start and finish belong together (mouse → cheese).

## Main character

| Key | Name | Look | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cat` | **Felix the cat** | A round-faced striped kitten: stripes on the forehead, oval eyes, whiskers, curly tail, sitting | Our own cat. Not the 1920s cartoon "Felix the Cat" (a trademarked black cat with a big white face), so keep him striped and kitten-like and never copy that design. |

## Props (goals)

`cheese`, `flower`, `star`, `cookie`, `house`, `moon`, `heart`, `tree`, `yarn`, `rosette`.

## Treats (collect along the way)

`crumb` (mouse, ants), `star` (Starry Night), `drop` (honeycomb, spider, when the cells are big enough), `leaf` (hedges), `heart` (dancing figure). Three sit on the right path and two at the ends of wrong turns, so exploring pays off too. Only on paths roomy enough to hold them.
