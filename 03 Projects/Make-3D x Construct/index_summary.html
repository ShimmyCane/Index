4. Adjacent variables: what they do and how to tweak them

These values change the look, the colours or the rules. Tweak a colour in css/app.css or js/viewer/viewer.js and reload. Change a rule in the engine or the world file, then rebuild the sample.

WhereNameValue nowWhat it does and how to tweak it
