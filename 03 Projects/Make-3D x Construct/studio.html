                Settings

                Off by default. When on, every error and every unexplained warning appears at the bottom, with its fix.

                Each item below needs a review by a person or an AI. Turn off what you do not need. AI reviews are done by copying a prompt into any AI chat and pasting the reply back; the page never calls an AI service.

                • At most 4 defects per round. Work a few things at a time, so you can tell which change did what.
                • Keep the best. A round that wins becomes the best version. A round that loses or ties is reverted, and the next round tries a different fix.
                • Stop after 2 rounds in a row without a win. Any win starts the count again.
                • No time cap. A session can run as long as it keeps winning.
                • A defect that stays stuck after three tries becomes a known limit. It is listed, not worked again.
                • Every criterion at the top score also ends the run. This needs scores, so it works only while the round scores are on (see Reviews).

                The limits are set in the code (4 and 2) and are the same for every run. The round cap is the only setting you can change here.
