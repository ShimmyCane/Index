# Consultation plan: the Southern Cheyenne camp move (draft)

Purpose: the camp move is about a specific nation. Before any display, the Cheyenne and Arapaho Tribes review it, and their decision is on record. This plan says who, what, how, and how each decision is written down. Nothing in this project is sent to anyone by the page or by Claude. Jimmy sends every message.

## 1. Who to ask

| Who | Why | Contact (to confirm first) |
|---|---|---|

Call the main office (listed as 405-262-0345, 100 Red Moon Circle, Concho, OK 73022) and ask for the THPO. Confirm the name, role, email and mailing address. Do not use a personal name from a directory unless the tribe confirms the person and role. Use the role in writing.

## 2. What to ask them

1. Should a camp move of this kind be shown at all? If no, the project stops here and the page stays closed.
2. Which parts are wrong, missing or not appropriate: the lodges and how the cover is folded; the travois and the load; the horses; the people and their clothing; the fire ring; the bluff and the ground.
3. Which story claims in `exhibit/story.json` (claims c1 to c7) can be supported, and by which sources they would accept.
4. Are there things the scene must not show (ceremonial objects, names, particular families or events)? The story already says none are shown; confirm it.
5. Is there a preferred way to show it: label wording, a credit line, and who may see it?
6. What conditions apply to any display, and can they ask for it to be taken down?

## 3. How (steps)

1. **Call first** to confirm the contact (section 1). Note the date and the name and role of the person spoken to in `private/consultation-log.md`.
2. **Send the draft letter** (`research/consultation-letter-draft.md`) with the four packet pictures and the story. Jimmy fills in the brackets and sends it. Nothing is sent by the page.
3. **Written review.** The reviewers answer section 2 in writing. Keep their answers in `private/` (not committed to the public repo).
4. **Meeting or visit** only if the reviewers ask for one.
5. **Record each decision** in the page: close each consultation flag with a note. The note says the role of the reviewer (not a personal name unless the tribe gives it), the date, and the outcome: `approved`, `changes requested`, or `declined`. The "consultation close note" switch in ⚙️ requires this note.
6. **Changes requested:** edit the scene or the story, rebuild the sample, and send the new packet back for review. Each new version gets new flags where needed.
7. **Declined:** the camp is not shown. Keep the project for study only, mark the page as not for display, and say so in the nursery schematic.

## 4. Rules that do not change

- Nothing is shown to the public while any consultation flag is open. The display gate in ⚙️ says so, and the header shows it as a red pill.
- The gate may be switched off in ⚙️ only for private study. The page then says "do not show this to the public".
- The tribe's answers are kept in `private/`, which is not committed. The public repo keeps only the decision (role, date, outcome).
- A declined or withdrawn review means the camp is not displayed, even if every flag is closed.
- The page does not call any AI or any server. AI checks (the fact-check and the critic) are copy-and-paste only, and their replies are kept only if they pass the checks.
