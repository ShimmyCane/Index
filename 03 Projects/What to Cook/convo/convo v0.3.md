# What to Cook — Convo v0.3

**Project:** What to Cook
**Round:** 3. Scope widened. Recorded as plans only; no tables added yet.

## New direction (from Jimmy)

1. The app can also hold **favourite restaurants**, **favourite brands** and **favourite meals**.
2. In general it compiles a **family food journal**: what we ate, where, what we thought of it, and what we'd cook again.
3. Concept name: "Digital Kitchen", "Twin Kitchen" or "Food Lab". The project is about the family really practising different cuisines (cooking, trying and comparing them).

## Working read (to confirm)

- The food journal is the spine. Restaurants, brands, home meals and recipe trials all become journal entries with a date, a who, a rating and a note.
- "Cuisine practice" is a tag on recipes, meals and restaurants (for example Thai, Mexican, Japanese), so the app can show what the family has tried per cuisine and what is still to try.
- Restaurants, brands and meals are favourites lists with a rating per family member, the same way recipe tastes work.
