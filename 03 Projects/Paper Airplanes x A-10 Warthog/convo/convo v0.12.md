# Convo v0.12 — the plane drawn properly

Date: 2026-11-18. Follows `convo v0.11.md`.

- The plane is no longer a small sprite stretched up. It is drawn at its own detail on a 66-unit grid: a broad body, a thick high wing, two fins set wide, two engines with glowing exhausts, and camouflage patches.
- It is painted at four crisp whole-step sizes, from about 26% to 33% of the screen width, with the turn choosing the size. Nothing is stretched.
- The stacking is built into the drawing: four slices, with the lower ones darker, so the body reads solid.
- The boost flame and the smoke marks follow the new drawing.

Still open: the look of the other sprites (tanks, buildings, aircraft) gets the same treatment in the next art pass, one at a time, drawn at their own size rather than enlarged.
