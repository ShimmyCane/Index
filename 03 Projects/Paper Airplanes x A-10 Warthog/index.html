📖 How it is built

No pictures are loaded. Each plane, tank and truck is a short list of coloured rectangles in src/sprites.ts. The page paints them once, adds a dark outline, and keeps the result.

Chase view: the camera sits behind the plane. The ground is a checkerboard in the world, drawn with perspective, so turning the plane turns the ground past it (src/render.ts).

The flight is in src/flight.ts: lift and drag grow with speed squared, lift also grows with the angle of attack, and past about 15° the lift falls away (a stall). Steering turns the heading and banks the wings.

Sound is made from a noise burst and tones (src/audio.ts).

Source is TypeScript in src/, compiled to js/game.js, which the page runs.
