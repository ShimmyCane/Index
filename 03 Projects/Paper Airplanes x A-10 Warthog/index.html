Settings

Fly the A-10 jet, or a paper glider through the rings, over a living world. Drag from the middle to steer, hold FIRE, MISSILE or CHAFF to act, and this panel pauses the game.

🎮 Controls (toggles)

Normal: limited ammo and missiles, and three hits takes the plane down. Missions restock you when the objective is done. Felix: the plane never goes down and nothing runs out.

Changing this starts a new flight.

Also V on a keyboard.

Drag: hold above the middle to climb, left of it to turn left. Joystick: a pad in the lower left.

🎛️ Other toggles
🛡️ Safety and reload

On: a floor above the ground. Near it the plane levels out and pulls up, so it cannot crash.

On: shells and missiles do no harm, and a crash only bounces the plane.

Automatic: a missile comes back every few seconds, up to six. Off: six missiles, then none.

👀 Show

Lift (green), weight (blue), thrust (orange), drag (red). Longer arrow, bigger push.

🔊 Sound

The warning beeps when an enemy shell comes close.

🛩️ Enemies and allies

A wave comes into your airspace about every minute. Set 0 for none.

Allies fly around their own patch and do not shoot. Hitting one costs points. Lost allies are replaced.

The game pauses while this panel is open.

📖 About

No pictures are loaded. Every plane, tank, house and tree is a small 3-D model made of tiny cubes (voxels), built in code in src/assets/models.ts.

Sprite stacking: each model is cut into thin layers. The layers are drawn one above another, turned to face the camera, so the model looks solid from every side and changes as you fly round it (src/voxel.ts).

The land and the sky are painted pixel by pixel: each pixel looks out along a ray and takes the colour of what it meets: fields, towns, roads, lakes, the sea, the sun, far mountains. Then everything fades into the haze (src/render.ts).

The flight is in src/flight.ts: lift and drag grow with speed squared, lift also grows with the angle of attack, and past about 15° the lift falls away (a stall).

Sound is made from tones and noise in the page (src/audio.ts).

Source is TypeScript in src/, compiled to js/game.js, which the page runs.
