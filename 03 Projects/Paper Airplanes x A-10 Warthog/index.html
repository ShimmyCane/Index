📖 How it is built

No pictures are loaded. Each plane, tank and truck is a short list of coloured rectangles in src/sprites.ts. The page paints them once, adds a dark outline, and keeps the result.

Every frame the game does four things: read the keys, move the plane, move the bullets and shells, and draw the sky, hills, ground and sprites back to front. The layers scroll at different speeds, which is what gives the flat side view depth.

The flight is in src/flight.ts: lift and drag grow with speed squared, lift also grows with the angle of attack, and past about 15° the lift falls away (a stall).

The sound is made from a noise burst and tones (src/audio.ts).

Source is TypeScript in src/. It is compiled to js/game.js, which is what the page runs.
