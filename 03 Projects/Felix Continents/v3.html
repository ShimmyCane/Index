📝 Notes for improvement
    About this page

    Version 0.5.0. The shaded areas are real boundaries from aourednik/historical-basemaps (GPL-3.0), simplified, one snapshot every 100 to 500 years. The source says to check its maps against other sources before academic use. Between snapshots the shapes blend, so they are not exact for the years in between.

    Before 100 CE there is no source yet, so this page starts at 100 CE.
