📝 Notes for improvement
    About this page

    Version 0.4.0. Today's coastlines are Natural Earth 1:50m land (public domain), simplified. The shapes of the past are rough sketches drawn by hand, not checked against a published reconstruction. Dates and positions are approximate.

    The climate is a simple model: latitude, distance from the sea, and mountains that catch wet winds. It is not a weather forecast.

    Built with three.js 0.170.0 (MIT licence).
