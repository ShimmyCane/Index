📝 Notes for improvement
    About this page

    Version 0.1.0 (first build, planning answers from convo v0.1 taken as proposed).

    The continent shapes are rough sketches drawn by hand, not checked against a published reconstruction. Dates and positions are approximate.

    The climate is a simple model: latitude, distance from the sea, and mountains that catch wet winds. It is not a weather forecast.

    Built with three.js 0.170.0 (MIT licence).
