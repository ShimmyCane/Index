🧭 Game flow

How one visit to the game goes, from opening it to the next house.

The steps

1. Pick a houseTap a house on the right. Locked houses show 🔒.
2. Pick a thingTap a thing like the bed. It comes up to the top of the room.
3. Tap the roomThe thing jumps to the spot you tapped. Walls for windows and pictures, floor for beds and sofas.
4. Move or keepTap a placed thing to pick it up, then tap the room. Tap it again to put it down where it is.
5. FinishWhen every thing for the house is placed, a card says "Lovely home!" and the next house opens.
