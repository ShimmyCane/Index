## Proposal (for Jimmy to answer)

**Credits (the bank).** A question round like the Test Bank: a topic, a question, a few answers. A right answer earns credits. A right answer on the first try, or a fast one, earns a bonus. The bank shows the total on screen before any shop opens. Questions are written by Claude from the topics Jimmy chooses, and kept in the project folder.

**Shops (Retail therapy).** Twelve or more, each with a short shelf of items and a price in credits. The four named shops are used as the first four; the other eight are generic shops (bakery, toy shop, pet shop, garden centre, bookshop, shoe shop, electronics, furniture). Each item is a matching emoji, as the house things are now. Nothing is copied from any real store's logo, packaging or product names.

**Matching (what the credits buy).** Items bought go into the bag. The bag works like the house list: items bought in a shop show up in the decorate buttons for the house, and an item from any shop can be placed in any house. Each house has the same mechanics as now: tap the item, tap the room.

**Going home.** After a set spend (for example, 12 shops visited and a total spent), the shop trip ends and Athena goes home with what she bought. She decorates with the new items. When she is happy, she can go back to the shops from the menu.

**Menu.** Home (decorate), Shops, Credits (questions), Job. Each phase keeps its own progress.

**Buy a job.** Not designed yet. It is the last step after the houses.
