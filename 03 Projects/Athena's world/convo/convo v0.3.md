## Answers from Jimmy

1. **Purpose:** a game about decorating homes. Athena builds and decorates houses, and later workplaces too.
2. **What Athena does:** decorates a room. She picks who it is for, places things, and moves them until she likes them.
6. **Kind:** a game, a play thing for the kids on iPad.
8. **MVP as described:**
   - A blank room, all white.
   - On the side, buttons to choose who the house is for (a house or a workplace).
   - On the side, decoration buttons: wall, window, bed (and more later).
   - Press a decoration and it appears up top. Tap the room and it goes there straight away.
   - It does not need a second tap to confirm. A thing that is in a good spot stays put.
   - To move something, tap it, then tap the new spot. Tap it again to put it down where it is.
   - When the house is finished, move on to the next house, so things can be built house by house.

## Assumptions I made (correct any inline)

- **Platform (Q7):** iPad, landscape first, portrait stacks the room above the buttons.
- **"Passing":** a house is finished when all its listed things are placed. Position is Athena's choice, so there is no right answer.
- **Houses in the MVP:** three, in order. Athena's house (bed, window, picture), Cosy house (bed, sofa, window), Workplace: the shop (window, picture, plant). Only the next house unlocks after one is finished.
- **Room colour (Q10):** the room is white, as asked. Buttons and the rest of the page follow the kids' pastel style.
- **Saving (Q9):** the unlocked houses are remembered in this browser only. Nothing else is saved yet.
- **Undo:** a prominent Undo, plus "Empty this house" in the ⚙️ panel.
- **Sound:** small synthesized sounds, which can be switched off in ⚙️.
