## The job flow (first design)

1. **Job board.** A row of job cards, six at a time. Each card shows the icon, the name and one line about the job. Tap a card to look at it.
2. **Job card.** A bigger card with what the job does and "A day at work" in three short pictures or steps. Buttons: **Apply** or **Back**.
3. **Learn** (after Apply). Two or three short pages about the job, each with one picture and a few words. A **Got it** button ends each page.
4. **Quiz.** Three to five multiple-choice questions about the pages. Wrong answers show a hint, and Athena can try again. No score is shown in red.
5. **Challenge.** A question for a real person who does this job (a grown-up or a worker Athena knows). It is a scenario, or a "What would you do?" or "What are some ways to solve this?". Scaffolded so Athena can answer:
   - a picture of the situation,
   - three choices to pick from,
   - sentence starters ("I would ___ because ___"),
   - a space to type or say her answer, saved as a note on the job.
6. **Work on it (first pass).** A short practice task that looks like the job, for example, "Build the wall of the cottage" for a builder. Done when Athena finishes the steps.
7. **Hired!** A badge is added to the job card. Athena can go back to the board and try another job.

Everything after step 1 is stored on the job, so the parts can be built later one job at a time.
