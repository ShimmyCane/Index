## Built this round, with defaults to check

- **Surrender:** a team outnumbered 10 to 1 gives up, when Surrender is on in ⚙️. Its spots go neutral with their soldiers, and its soldiers on the march are lost. The computers and you both follow the rule.
- **Upgrade times:** town 3 seconds, city 6, metropolis 10, fortress 10. The army does not grow while a spot upgrades. The cost is paid at the start. A capture cancels the upgrade, and the cost is lost. A dust puff shows the upgrade.
- **Levels 10 to 15:** multiplayer only. Four to six flags, 30 to 52 spots, on a field two screens wide. Team sizes stay fair.
- **Zoom and minimap:** + and −, Fit, and two-finger pinch. The minimap shows only when the zoomed view scrolls. Tap or drag it to move the view.
