## Questions

1. **City cap and upgrades.** What should the cap limit: the number of soldiers a city can hold, the city's growth rate, or the number of upgrades? What does an upgrade cost (soldiers, time, or nothing), and what does the top upgrade unlock?

2. **Team mode.** How are teams set up: picked before the game, or assigned at random? Should teammates share a colour, or keep their own colour with a team badge? Should a team win when all of its members' rivals are gone?

3. **Dragging to a spot without a road.** When a drop lands on a region that has no road from the dragged spot, should soldiers route along roads through your own spots, or should the drop be refused (as now)? If routing, should soldiers march the whole way, or should only the first step move?

4. **Team avatars.** Which animals? Should the avatar replace the mushroom icon on the map, or appear only on the team card? (Your note also says "Each team should be their own. Not all fungi.")

5. **Hard mode balance.** In a test on Hard, the computer won in about 20 seconds against a player who did nothing. Is that the level you want, or should Hard be a bit slower? Play two or three full games and tell me how it felt.

6. **Feedback that is not yet in the table.** The notes on your device sync once you sign in from ⚙️ → Family database and tap "Send my maps and notes." Did that happen on the device where you wrote them?
