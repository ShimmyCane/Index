# Felix Gear Wars — convo v0.4

**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Asked by:** Claude · **Answers:** Jimmy, inline after each `A:`

Round 4 is built (version 0.4.0). Your list, and how I read it:

1. **Boss gear.** It needs 100 heart turns for one turn of its own (1 to 100 against other gears), shown as a slow-turning purple gear with a **crown inside**. When it completes, a **champion** steps out: a giant of the newest fighter of the chapter, with a crown over its head. ⚙️ **Boss gear ratio** (10 to 200, default 100) changes the number. Merging two boss gears makes the champion stronger and the wait 25% shorter. It costs 120 coins and appears in the tray from chapter 2 (rarely), and always in free-for-all.
2. **Heart speed.** Half the old pace: one turn every 3.2 seconds. The ⚙️ slider still goes from ×0.5 to ×4 on top of that.
3. **Smooth.** The heart turns without a beat or tick, and the gears' progress rings fill continuously. Gears ease to their meshing angle instead of snapping. The tick sound and heart pulse are gone.
4. **At most 50 soldiers on each side.** A gear at the limit waits with its charge full; enemies queue at their fortress. The HUD shows "Army n/50".
5. **Flourish for every gear level.** Level 2: gold rim and studs. Level 3: gold-tinted gear with jewels. Level 4: golden rays around the teeth and twinkles. Fighters show the same level: level 2 a silver ring and one star, level 3 a gold ring, a glow, gold-tinted armour and two stars, level 4 a pulsing ring, a stronger glow and three stars. Stats still rise 2.2× per level.
6. **Map width.** Chapters 1 to 3: one screen. Chapters 4 and 5: one and a half screens. Chapters 6 to 8 and free-for-all: two screens.
7. **Castle defences.** Each castle (yours and the enemy fortress) has a stone-thrower on its wall and shoots arrows and stones at the other side's soldiers in range.
8. **Towers** from chapter 4 (the first level where the map is long enough): each side builds two, at 10% and 25% of the way across. The near one shoots arrows; the far one throws stones. Enemy soldiers and yours stop to attack towers on their way. A destroyed tower is rubble, then a scaffold that grows, and it is back after 45 seconds.

## Questions

1. Is a champion every 100 heart turns (about 5 minutes at normal speed with one chain) the right wait? Shall the champion be a giant of the newest fighter, or something else (a dragon, a cart)?
   A: A dragon sounds awesome, but in reality the boss gear should give a random, awesome, different unit: dragon, king, cart, Felix the Cart, and a few others, each with their own amazing powers.
2. Are the castles' arrows and stones strong enough to feel useful, and not so strong that they play the game for him?
   A: They should be the equivalent of 5 to 10 units, depending on the level.
3. Tower rebuild time is 45 seconds. Shorter or longer? Should a tower come back at full health or half?
   A: OK: only rebuild when the defence is able to push the enemy back beyond that space.
4. Should towers also appear in chapters 1 to 3, scaled for one screen, or stay a later surprise?
   A: No.
