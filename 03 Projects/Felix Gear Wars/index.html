Settings

How it works. Every level is shaped like a hill. Waves start gentle, the most exciting wave comes at the fun peak, and then waves ease off and stop at the fade time. After that the Horde loses heart and its fortress slowly crumbles, so a level lasts about as long as the fade time.

Each wave is sized from how many gears and fighters you have right now, plus what your gears will make before the wave arrives. Lots of gears means a bigger wave, so it stays exciting but never unfair. Challenge makes every wave a little softer or harder. The small curve at the bottom left of the game shows where you are on the hill.

Each tap adds 25 coins in a level. Balance test without playing: node tools/balance_sim.js
