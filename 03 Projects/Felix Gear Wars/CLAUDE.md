# Felix Gear Wars — Claude Code pointer

Purpose: a gear-powered lane defense game for Felix. A turning heart gear drives the gears he places on a pegboard; each gear makes a fighter when it turns, and the fighters drop to the battlefield and march to stop waves of enemies before they reach the base. Campaign: 8 chapters × 3 levels, each chapter adds a new fighter.

Track: A. Family play
