# Felix Gear Wars — story plan

## Premise
The kingdom of **Cogsworth** is kept alive by the **Great Heart**, a clockwork heart that ticks and turns every gear in the land. The **Rust Horde** (the enemy, in rust red with odd hats, scarves and boots) wants the Heart to stop. The player is the **Keeper**, who places gears; each gear makes a defender. The Rust King is the end of the road in chapter 8.

## Plot line
1. The Heart wakes after a hundred years. First gear, first spearmen. A scout party arrives. The first boss, the Pike Lord, tests the road.
2. The Wood has archers who sing to the Heart. The Whistle Thief steals their song to hide the Horde's march.
3. Hill smiths forge brass and shields. Lightning gears are found in an old tower. Baron Brassback wears stolen shields.
4. Fog hides the marsh road. Quiet assassins join. Fogblade wants to cut the Heart's chain.
5. A wide plain, the first horses. Shuriken gears roll like wheels. Captain Clatter leads a rusty cavalry.
6. The mountain pass: a giant wall of armour. Heavy tanks join; magic is what armour fears.
7. The Star Tower: mages read the sky. Rockets are lit like fireworks. The Ash Wizard tries to dim the stars.
8. The Rust Throne: catapults on both sides. The Rust King is a rusted copy of the Great Heart's first keeper. The Keeper turns the last gear.
