1. Level five too slow; each level should take no more than about 10 fish. **Built:** each fish meal gives at least a ninth of the level's size range, so a level takes at most about nine meals. This replaces the 1/10 slowdown from v0.6.
2. Higher levels: schools of your own species follow you and attack other fish; feeding the group chum makes them reproduce and level up. **Built:** a pod of your species from level 10 (2 to 12 members), which attacks smaller fish and grows with you. Eating chum near the pod gives a 50% chance of a new member.
