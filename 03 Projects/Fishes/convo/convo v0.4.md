## What was built (v0.5)

- Phones are 2.5× larger; pinch and double-tap zoom off.
- Settings: "Health bars" and "Controls" (tap and move, or joystick at the lower left).
- Sprint moved to the lower-right action column, just above ⚙️.
- A bite timer ring around you; red splashes on bites; wounded fish draw attackers.
- Livelier swimming.
- Eating gives 25% of prey size (was 40%), so levels take longer.
- Bigger levels: the world grows faster, fish get faster with each tier, and the sea holds more fish.
- Level up: old fish stay; new fish swim in from the edges and can't touch you for six seconds.
- ⚙️ End game shows score and best.
- Infinite mode on the title screen.
