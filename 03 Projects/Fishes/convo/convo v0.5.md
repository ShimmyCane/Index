## What was built (v0.6)

- Level up keeps the fish around you.
- Growth from level 2 is 1/10 as fast. Level 1 keeps its pace, with more plankton.
- Fish of size 4 or more take 2 to 5 bites.
- Schools defend: bitten school fish are backed up by their schoolmates.
- Predators 2.5× a fish's size make it scatter from further away.
- Predators gather at chum.
- Dead larger fish break into chum bits instead of vanishing.
