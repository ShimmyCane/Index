Fishes

Start as a baby fish. Eat, grow, and become the largest fish ever.

  • Hold your finger on the sea to swim toward it. (Arrow keys work on a computer.)
  • Hold 💨 Sprint to dash. It uses stamina and comes back when you stop.
  • Eat plankton and smaller fish. Bigger fish will eat you.
  • A red ring means a hunter has locked on to you. Swim away or grow bigger.
  • Reach size 100 on level 20 to win. You have 3 lives and 15 minutes.
