# Ondra — Creatures, spirits and forgotten things

For each: what it looks like, what it does, how to meet it, and a note on how it feels to a child (wonder, funny, a little spooky, sad).

---

## 1. The Wardens — the great spirits

Sung from the Tree's first seven leaves, one for each part of the world. Huge, old, slow to wake. Seeing one is the event of a lifetime.

| Warden | Land | Looks like | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Dawn Stag** | The Greenhollow | A white stag with antlers like the branches of a young tree, leaves and all; flowers grow in its hoofprints | Has not been seen for fifty years. Bristle may be its messenger. |
| **The Great Heron** ("Old Stillness") | The Mirrorlakes | A grey heron as tall as a house, standing on one leg in the mist | Keeper of reflections and memory. Saw everything on the Night Without Moon. |
| **The Salt Serpent** | The Ashen Reach | Once the River Serpent, blue and green and endless. Now white with salt, asleep under the great dune | Its snoring is the dunes' song. Dreams of being a river. |
| **The Wind Mare** | The Tallgrass | A silver mare you see only as grass bending in a running line | Has had a foal this year: a sign. |
| **The Stone Bear** | The Stairstep Mountains | A mountain shaped like a sleeping bear | Rolls over once a century (an earthquake). |
| **The Aurora Owl** | The White North | An owl with wings of green light | Carries the dead's last thoughts to the Tree's crown. |
| **The Deep Mother** | The sea | A whale bigger than an island, singing | Sings the Wayfinders' star-paths. |

**The eighth: the Hush.** The spirit of rest and forgetting, left out of the songs. Looks like soft grey snow with pale moths in it, or a tall shape made of fog with a face like someone you almost remember. Where it drifts, small things are forgotten. Not evil, but wounded since Orrin the Bright bound it. *Feels: sad and quiet, never jump-scary. It wants to be thanked for the good forgetting it does.*

## 2. The forest

- **Whisperfox.** A fox with autumn-red fur and a silver-tipped tail. It repeats the last thing you said, in your own voice, a little slyly. When it repeats a lie it laughs, so foxes are good lie-detectors. Leaves when it gets bored. *Funny, cheeky.*
- **Grandmother Owl.** The old grey owl in the hollow oak. Always asks "Who? Whooo?" She is really asking. She knows the names of everyone who has ever slept in the forest, and gives a name back to anyone who earns it. *Wise, a little stern.*
- **Mossback deer.** Gentle giant deer, shoulder-high to a horse, with moss and ferns growing on their backs. Shy. A sick one, grey-eyed from nightbloom, is a Book 1 hook. *Wonder.*
- **Thornhog.** A grumpy hedgehog the size of a dog, with quills like twigs. Snores. Rolls into a ball and blocks paths. Loves apples. *Funny.*
- **Lanternmoths.** Moths that glow soft gold. Follow the Hush and light up what it touched, so they help find lost things. *Wonder.*
- **Linden bees.** Fat gold bees from the linden trees. They talk in dances, and Nettle understands them. They know every flower, and every footprint near one.
- **Bristle.** The scruffy grey wild dog, one ear up, one flopped, who guarded the Seedling's sleep. Never barks, only growls, sniffs, wags and looks. Notices smells and sounds first. Growls at nightbloom and at liars. Nobody knows how old he is. (Storyteller only: Bristle may be the Dawn Stag's messenger, or a Rootkin-made guardian. Keep it a mystery a long while.)
- **Pip the crow.** Granny Wren's crow. Steals shiny things, says three words ("Wren!", "Mine!", "Bread!").

## 3. Spirits of house and wood

- **Hearthwisps.** House spirits. A little flame-coloured creature, the size of a kitten, that lives behind the stove. Fed a bowl of milk and a crust each night. Keeps the bread rising and the fire friendly. When the family quarrels, it sulks, and the bread goes flat. Mends with an apology. *Inspired by Slavic house spirits. Cosy.*
- **Grandfather Moss.** The forest lord: an old man made of moss and bark, as tall as a tree when he wants, as small as a stump when he doesn't. Leads rude travellers in circles and polite ones home. Loves riddles, hates axes. *A little spooky, mostly grumpy-kind.*
- **Mire-lights.** Small blue lights over the Mirefen. Each is a memory the Hush took that never found its owner. Follow one and it may lead you to the person it belongs to. *Spooky-beautiful.*
- **Stone-wakers.** The Carved Stones are slightly alive. They lean towards Rootkin and Seedlings.

## 4. The lakes

- **Mirror carp.** Golden fish with scales like mirrors. Look into one's side and you see a memory: yours, or the lake's. The Lumai keep them in jars as oracles. *Wonder.*
- **Stillhorse.** A grey horse of water and weed that stands in the shallows and invites you to ride. Climb on and it runs into the lake. Says thank you to it, and it carries you safely across instead. *Inspired by northern water horses. Spooky, then kind.*
- **Reed-sprites.** Tiny green creatures who braid reeds and tangle fishing lines for fun.
- **The Great Heron** (Warden).

## 5. The desert

- **Sail-eared foxes.** Tiny pale foxes with ears bigger than their heads. Hear water underground. *Cute.*
- **Longwalkers.** The caravan camels. Each one has a name and a personality, and refuses to move until you say it.
- **Singing dunes.** When the sand slides, the dune hums deep, like a giant cello. The Salt Serpent snoring.
- **Glasswights.** Spirits born where lightning strikes sand and makes glass. Shimmering, quick, clever, love bargains and riddles, keep promises exactly to the word. *Inspired by desert spirits of story. Tricky, never wicked.*
- **Ash-beetles.** Black beetles that roll balls of ash; they are slowly turning the Glass Flats back into soil.
- **Glass Golems.** The Glass Kings' guardians: tall figures of black glass, asleep half-buried on the Glass Flats. Wake at the Reckoning. *A little scary; save for later books.*

## 6. Mountains, grass, north and sea

- **Condor-kings.** Huge mountain birds that carry messages for the Yanari.
- **Shags.** Woolly, long-necked mountain beasts; carry loads; spit when offended.
- **Sky-eagles.** The clans' hunting eagles, each bonded to one rider for life.
- **Snow-elk** of the north, with antlers that chime in the cold.
- **Song-seals.** Seals who sing the Wayfinders home in fog.

## 7. Dark things

Used sparingly, told gently (`bible.md`, section 8).

- **Nightbloom.** A black flower with a silver centre, growing only where forest was burned (the Glass Flats, and wherever the Ash Hand plants it). Its scent brings the **grey sleep**: eyes go grey, the sleeper dreams of nothing and cannot wake. Cures (to be quested for): honey from the linden bees mixed with water from the Singing Stream; a mirror carp's scale; a song from someone who loves the sleeper. Bristle growls at it; the key goes cold near it.
- **The Unremembered.** Pale, quiet shapes of people the world forgot, standing at the edges of rooms. They do not hurt anyone. They want to be named. Saying a name, any name, kindly, lets one rest or begin to come back. *Sad more than scary.*
- **The sun-fire.** The Glass Kings' bottled sunlight, sealed inside the Tree's heart. Beautiful, and it burns everything it touches.
- **Orrin the Bright.** The last Glass King. Vanished on the Night Without Moon. The Glass Remnant think he sleeps inside a block of sun-glass in the Drowned Quarter. *Not for Book 1.*
- **The Ash Hand's grey moths.** Moths dusted with nightbloom pollen, sent to make a guard sleep.

## 8. The forgotten

See also `peoples.md`, section 10.

- **The Rootkin** — the first people; fading glimmers. The quiet heart of Book 1.
- **The Moon's Children** — under the lakes.
- **The Green House** — the erased desert family.
- **The River Serpent** — what the Salt Serpent was, and could be again.
- **The Seventh song** — the Wardens' songs are sung everywhere with one verse missing. It is the Hush's verse. Finding it is a long thread across books.
