# Bar Glide — convo v0.1 (2026-11-09)

## Jimmy's request (summary)
Start a project for an old bartender arcade game he played (maybe on the Atari too): a bartender tends three or more long bars, customers move slowly up the bars, he pours and slides mugs to them, they drink and slide the empties back, and he must catch them before they break. A little "uh-oh" when a glass breaks. Three breaks and the bar owner comes in, bends him over the bar and spanks him, with little pixel tears. The mood, colour and atmosphere matter most; it's nostalgic. Set it up with the settings button, notes, database and version control. Merge.

## What Claude did (round 1)
- Built the MVP straight away, since Jimmy asked to merge.
- Named it **Bar Glide**. "Tapper" is the original game's trademarked title, and Claude only builds originals in a genre, not copies. The mechanics are the genre's; the name, characters, art and sound are new.
- Default mood: a late-night saloon (dark wood, brass, lamps, neon). The pastel day room is in ⚙️.
- The spanking ending is in, as asked, as a slapstick cartoon. ⚙️ has a gentler "telling off" ending for when the kids play.
