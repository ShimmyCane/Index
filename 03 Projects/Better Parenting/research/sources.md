## What the research says, in short
- Gottman: parents who coach emotions follow all five steps only about a fifth to a quarter of the time. Perfection is not the standard.
- Kazdin: praise for the behaviour you want does most of the teaching; punishment teaches little.
- Greene: the first Plan B talk can be only the empathy step; the rest can wait a day.
- Lebowitz: nearly all parents of anxious children accommodate; reducing it step by step, with support, works about as well as therapy for the child.
- AAP: for ages 5 to 10 there is no single hour limit; look at what screens crowd out. No own social media accounts yet.
- Kennedy: repair matters more than the rupture: name it, own it, say what you will do differently.
