Purpose:
The nursery for small programs. Each one is a seed that starts here. When it matures, Jimmy copies it into its own repo by hand and builds it up there. Projects are independent of each other.

Per-project layout:
- schematic.md — design and requirements
- convo/ — clarifying Q&A, one file per round (`convo vX.Y.md`), never compacted
- CLAUDE.md — short pointer file for Claude Code (added by `/start-project` or `/claude-review-project`)
- the app or code itself
- AI notes live in `04 AI/<Project>/`

Status key (set one per project):
idea → MVP → device-tested → in use → graduated (copied to its own repo) → retired

Schematic:
- 中文笔画/ — status: MVP 0.1.0 (not yet device-tested by a child). A kids' iPad game for the worksheet characters: the child sees the pinyin and writes each character stroke by stroke. A wrong stroke is corrected on the spot; after 2 misses the answer is shown and its stroke order plays. Hints match 汉字数独 3.0. Weekly words go in `data/batches.json`, one tab per upload date (newest first). Track A. Traditional Chinese throughout. Questions in `convo/convo v0.1.md` to `convo/convo v0.3.md`. (`index.html`, `js/`, `css/`, `data/`, `tools/`, `schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `convo/`)
- Adventure/ — status: MVP (not yet device-tested). A family story game played like correspondence chess, with a nightly AI storyteller. Player page `index.html` (voiced chapters with a recorded cast, sound effects and music borrowed from Voice), world tools `world.html`, lore reader `lore.html`, voice test page `voices.html`, under-the-hood `hood.html`, storyteller `storyteller.md`, SQL in `supabase/`, `todo.md`. Athena's story: world bible of Ondra, Book 1 and a ten-book plan in `world/`; chapter 1 voiced in `story/` + `audio/`; recorder `tools/make_voice.py` (also records the storyteller's chapters into a private bucket). (`schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `convo/`)
- Living Room Scene/ — status: MVP. A flat, pastel scene rebuilt from one living-room photo over ten iterations (`living_room_v1.html` to `living_room_v10.html`), each with a render in `renders/` and a contact sheet `renders/progress_sheet.png`. The photo is not in the repo. Log in `04 AI/Living Room Scene/`. (`schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `convo/`, `renders/`)
- Shimmy Specs/ — status: idea. A web app that guides the process of writing CSI specs: project summary, code analysis, keynotes and PPD drive the section list; each section gets a schematic of questions; decisions keep their alternatives and impacts; then a three-part draft. Body of knowledge in `knowledge/`, goals in `todo.md`, MVP design in `mvp schematic.md`, build list for a Haiku session in `mvp tasks.md`. Round 1 answered (`convo/convo v0.2.md`); five questions open. (`schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `convo/`)
- Critique Detail/ — status: MVP 0.2.0. A results page and a script around a written review of one architectural detail: a CSI rulebook library (57 detail types, 22 written, the rest stubs), a QC list built for each detail from the rulebook, project conditions, mandates and lessons, a hard gate that rejects unreadable drawings, a report and a redlined image. The code calls no AI service; Claude writes reviews in a session (`reviewer.md`). Lean proof ideas (verdict, proof ledger, control layers, good-detail pictures, one finding at a time) are toggles in ⚙️. Track B. Architecture database, tables `dc_*` (001 live; 002 written, not applied). Questions in `convo/convo v0.2.md`. (`run_review.py`, `reviewer.md`, `schemas/`, `library/`, `inputs/`, `outputs/`, `index.html`, `js/`, `css/`, `supabase/`, `tools/`, `schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `todo.md`, `convo/`)
- Rhythm Sticks/ — status: MVP 0.3.1 (not yet device-tested). A wordless web toy that exposes the kids to rhythm and sound. A random mode loads each time: ripples, beads (microphone beat looper), hidden shake pads, strings, two-finger span, "how the machine hears" (a microphone heat map), echo, marbles, dot loop, footsteps and hum trail. A hidden breathing garden (sand rake, fountain, blossom) opens from the gear panel. Hidden ⚙️ opens with a circle in the lower-right. Notes for improvement go to the family table `rs_notes` (SQL written, not applied). Track A. Shared background in `context.md`; questions in `convo/convo v0.8.md`. (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `supabase/`, `context.md`, `schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `convo/`)
- Generate Clouds/ — status: MVP 0.3.0 (orbit around a point in the clouds with a self-turning view; zoom to 35× with a sharp redraw of still views, height to 400 km, cities sorted by current weather; headless-tested with made-up weather; the live weather link and iPad not yet tried). Pick a place and a moment; the page reads the weather (Open-Meteo: cloud by height, wind, storms, temperature, air quality) and generates a 3-D volumetric sky with the sun in its real position, a 15-minute time lapse, a 360° look-around from the ground to above the clouds, and PNG exports (a view up to 4K, an equirectangular panorama up to 8K) as backdrops for renderings. A reading panel explains each sky in plain words; a cloud atlas shows 13 sample skies. Track B. Device storage only. Background in `research/`; questions in `convo/convo v0.1.md` and `convo/convo v0.2.md`. (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `research/`, `schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `todo.md`, `convo/`)
- Felix Gear Wars/ — status: MVP 0.5.0 (headless-tested; not device-tested). A gear-powered lane defense game for Felix: a turning heart gear drives gears on a pegboard, each gear makes a fighter that drops to the battlefield and fights the Rust Horde. Merge equal gears for stronger, faster ones; multiplier and special gears (lightning, shuriken, rockets); eight fighters with counters; 8 chapters × 3 levels with a boss each third level, a free-for-all level, a two-screen battlefield with an enemy fortress, a story card per level in three reading tiers, eight cultures (French, Spanish, Aztec, Indian, Japanese, Chinese, Persian, Egyptian), each with its own look, fighter names, headgear and blades and one ★ specialty fighter with +25% (Japanese Ninja, French Chevalier...), plus three storybook looks, narrated story cards (Kokoro clips in `audio/`), synthesized sound and music, a crown boss gear, castles and towers that shoot arrows and stones, a 50-soldier cap, smooth gears, gears balanced by power (the catapult is three times slower than the spearman), and a headless balance test (`tools/balance_sim.js`). Notes go to the family table `fgw_notes` (SQL written, not applied). Track A. Questions in `convo/convo v0.1.md` (answered) and `convo v0.2.md`. (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `tools/`, `research/`, `supabase/`, `schematic.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `todo.md`, `convo/`)
