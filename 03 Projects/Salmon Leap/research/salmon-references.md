# Salmon Leap: references

Gathered 2026-11-18 for the leaping Atlantic salmon statue. Source strength is noted. Popular sites are weaker than a fisheries document.

## Body and proportions

- The body is spindle-shaped (fusiform), widest in the middle, tapering at both ends, somewhat flattened on the sides. Depth is roughly a quarter of the total length. The head and eyes are small. Sources: Maine Department of Marine Resources fact sheet (https://www.maine.gov/dmr/sites/maine.gov.dmr/files/docs/atlanticsalmon.pdf); Fishbase summary (https://fishbase.org/summary/236). Strength: good.
- The Atlantic Salmon Trust anatomy sheet gives the fin layout (https://atlanticsalmontrust.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/atlantic_salmon_anatomy.pdf). Strength: good.

## Fins and tail

- The dorsal and anal fins mainly stabilise the fish; the pectoral and pelvic fins steer. Source: Atlantic Salmon Trust (above). Strength: good.
- The caudal (tail) fin is the main source of propulsion; the tail is large and slightly forked, and helps the leap. Sources: SPS Salmon Education Group (https://spsseg.org/salmon-anatomy/); The Animal Facts (https://www.theanimalfacts.com/fish/atlantic-salmon/). Strength: moderate. The link between tail shape and leaping is described in general terms, not measured.
- The adipose fin is a small fleshy flap between the dorsal and the tail fin; its function is still debated. Source: Wildlife Informer (https://wildlifeinformer.com/?p=17630). Strength: moderate.

## Leaping

- Atlantic salmon are known for leaping at waterfalls on the way to the spawning grounds. Source: The Canadian Encyclopedia (https://encyclopediecanadienne.ca/article/atlantic-salmon). Strength: good.
- Leaps over 3 m (about 10 ft) at falls, weirs and other obstacles, and adults above 20 kg. Source: Ireland's Wildlife (https://irelandswildlife.com/?p=722). Strength: moderate.

## Sculpture

- Public works of leaping or rising salmon exist. A bronze of a jumping coho is sold by a US wildlife gallery (https://wildlifewonders.com/salmon-bronze-sculpture-coho-rising). Leaping salmon bronzes are sold by UK makers (for example, Leaping Salmon by Ian Greensitt: https://www.houseofbruar.com/leaping-salmon-by-ian-greensitt-an60021/). Strength: product listings, weak for design. Use for the idea of a bronze and a rising pose, not for measurements.
- No public waterfall statue was found in the search. The design here is original.

## Gaps

- No measured source for fin shape in a leap. The fin shapes in V1 to V5 are design choices, marked as such in the build.
- The scale is a choice: a 25 in statue. Real salmon length is not the statue's scale.
