# Salmon Leap: Convo v0.1

**Round:** 1 (started with the request: a leaping Atlantic salmon statue, five versions).

## What you asked

- A statue of an Atlantic salmon jumping up.
- Five versions: V1 narrative and rough massing; V2 narrative, light, colour, texture, background set-up; V3 concept and details, seen from all angles; V4 details increment; V5 the ground.
- Later: V10 movement, rendering and atmosphere; V15 sound.
- Save references to lessons learned.

## What was built

- V1 to V5 (15, 17, 26, 32, 40 parts). Each passes the audit. Page: `index.html` with the versions, narrative, views (front, side, back, top, oblique), colour modes.
- References: `research/salmon-references.md`. Lessons: `04 AI/Salmon Leap/lessons.md`.
- Name: "Salmon Leap" (I chose it; the project name was not given).

## Questions

1. **Scale and material.** The statue is 25 in long. Is that the size you want, and is bronze the right material (with a patina), or would you rather stone?
2. **The pose.** The arc here is a clean spine with the tail low and the nose up-forward. Do you want the fish turned to show more of its side, or arched more steeply?
3. **The base.** Three granite steps and a pillar under the belly. Is a waterfall the setting (V5 has a pool), or a stone step?
4. **V10 and V15.** When you want movement, rendering and atmosphere, and sound: tell me what the movement is (a flick of the tail, the leap itself) and what the sound is (water, the statue's bronze ring, a street).
