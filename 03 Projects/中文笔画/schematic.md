# 中文筆畫 — Schematic

Status: MVP 0.1.0 (built, not yet device-tested by a child)
Track: A. Family play (the child plays; ⚙️ holds everything secondary)

## Purpose
A kids' iPad game for the characters on the weekly 中文 worksheet. The child sees the pinyin and writes the character with a finger or Apple Pencil, stroke by stroke. Wrong strokes are corrected on the spot. After a set number of misses the right answer is shown, its stroke order plays, and the child writes it again. The tracing and hints match 汉字数独 3.0.

## Decisions (confirmed by Jimmy in convo v0.2)
- Readings confirmed: 黃 huáng, 青 qīng, 請 qǐng.
- Weekly uploads are grouped by upload date, one tab per date, newest first. The first batch is "2026-11-10 · 第一課".
- Teaching: when the child misses too often, correct them and show the right answer. Default 2 misses (⚙️ can set 1, 2 or 3). A character whose answer was shown can earn at most one star.
- Stroke hint is the same as 汉字数独 3.0: 💡 marks the next stroke, 🙋 says its name, ▶ 筆順 plays the whole stroke order.
- All page text in traditional Chinese; pinyin always shown.

## Data
- `data/batches.json`: the one file edited each week (`date`, `lesson`, `items` of `ch` and `py`).
- `data/batches.js` and `data/strokes.js`: generated by `tools/build_batches.py` (classic scripts, so the page works from a file).
- `tools/test_data.js`: checks the batches and the stroke coverage.
- `assets/fonts/wenkai-subset.woff2`: a subset of LXGW WenKai built by `tools/build_font.py`, covering the batch characters and the page text.

## Screens
1. **Main (tracing)**: date tabs across the top; the writing board with the stroke dots; the big character with pinyin and stroke count; buttons 🔊 讀音, 🙋 幫忙, 💡 提示, ▶ 筆順, 👀 答案, ↩️ 重寫, ⏭ 下一個字; the lesson's characters as a picker with stars.
2. **Celebration** after each character: stars, a short grade, 再寫一次 or 下一個字.
3. **⚙️ drawer**: 筆順提示, 寫錯幾次給答案, 動畫速度, 朗讀字音, 筆畫名, 聲音, 顯示錯誤 (off by default), 給我們的改進意見 (notes box), 寫過的字, version and changelog.

## Stars
- 3 stars: every stroke right the first time, no hints.
- 2 stars: at most two misses or hints in total.
- 1 star: more misses or hints, or the answer was shown.
- Stored per character on the device (local storage, in try/catch). ⚙️ can clear them.

## Feedback notes
"給我們的改進意見" in ⚙️, built from `02 Refs/Skills/feedback_notes.md`. Each note is saved with the date tab and character it is about. Notes stay on the device and are never sent anywhere.

## Data storage
No database. Progress and notes stay on the device.
