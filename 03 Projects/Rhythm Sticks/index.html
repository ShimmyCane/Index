Tap to drop a ripple. Hold to keep the note ringing, and slide to change it.

Nine hidden pads. Tap them, and drag across them. Look and listen to find what each one does.

Drag a finger across a string to pluck it. With two or more strings, touch the right side of a string to press a fret: sliding left raises the pitch.

Two fingers on the big side: the distance between them is the note. Two fingers on the small side: the distance is the loudness. Lift your fingers and the note keeps playing.

What the microphone hears, as a picture: time runs left to right, high sounds are at the top, loud sounds are hot colors. Tap to freeze.

  1. Tap the screen once to start recording. Clap, tap or drum near the microphone: each sharp sound becomes a bead.
  2. Tap again to stop. The beads loop: the loop is as long as your recording.
  3. Tap again to add another layer. It records for one turn of the loop, and each layer has its own sound and color.
  4. Tap again during a layer to finish it early.
  5. Double tap to wipe everything and start a new recording.

Your voice and steady noise are ignored; only sharp sounds count. The loop plays soft synth drums, never the recorded sound. The microphone is never stored or sent. If beads show up too often or not at all, change the sensitivity above.
