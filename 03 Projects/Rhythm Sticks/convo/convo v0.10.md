## Answers

1. **Microphone rest (Echo):** yes to both. The mic rests after 30 seconds with no sound or touch and a tap wakes it. When the browser says the mic is busy, the page names the other app or tab that may be using it.
2. **Dot loop:** yes to both. New rings grow outward and the others shrink to a dash-and-dot marker. The middle needs two taps to clear, with a "tap again" hint for 2 seconds.
3. **Two fingers, new design:** "I love it." Taken as my proposal, as written in v0.9:
   - triangle: a three-note chord,
   - square: a steady four-beat pulse,
   - circle: one long sustained note,
   - a drawn line: a melody along its height,
   - trails stay on screen and fade,
   - the split screen goes; a second pair of fingers sets the volume (closer = softer, further = louder).
4. **Two fingers, range and colour:** yes. About three octaves across the screen, and colour across the whole range.
5. **What each mode does:** yes. Jimmy noticed the duplicate. There will be one panel that explains the mode you are in, and the duplicate goes.
